(a) Group Project (25%)

The Group Project requires students to apply STEAM and/or AI technologies to design and

develop a functional prototype that addresses a real-world problem through the Engineering

Design Process.

Students are expected to produce:

o A working prototype

o A presentation documenting the design and development process

o A user/instructional manual of the prototype

o A live demonstration of the prototype

The report and presentation should clearly illustrate each stage of the Engineering Design Process

using appropriate evidence, such as flowcharts, diagrams, photographs, screenshots, videos, or

animations. Students should explain how the prototype operates, how it addresses the identified

problem, and the challenges encountered during development. Testing procedures, experimental

findings, and design modifications should also be discussed.

A live classroom demonstration is required. The prototype should be fully functional and available

for testing by classmates during the presentation session.

Suggested content:

The presentation can follow the steps of the engineering design process to illustrate how a dailylife problem can be solved through educational technology.

Problem Identification (15%)

o Clearly define the problem being addressed.

o Provide relevant background information and project objectives.

o Explain the significance of the problem and its impact on society or the target user

group.

Research and Analysis (15%)

o Present the research conducted to understand the problem, user needs, and existing

solutions.

o Analyze findings and identify key design requirements.

o Include supporting evidence from the Engineering Design Process Booklet, such

as screenshots, notes, or photographs, research articles, or news articles.

Prototype Design and Justification (15%)

o Describe the ideation and brainstorming process.

o Present alternative design concepts considered.

o Justify the selection of the final design.

o Highlight the key features and innovations of the proposed solution.

Prototyping and Evaluation (15%)

o Describe the construction and testing process.

o Present evaluation results, challenges encountered, and modifications made.

o Discuss how the solution addresses the identified problem and meets user needs.

o Include both hand-drawn sketches and 3D representations of the prototype.
